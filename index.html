Our principles

How we build

Three principles behind every Lanternbit Labs product, from the first release to the latest update.

Fair play

No rigged difficulty and no tactics that push you towards watching an ad.

Respect for your time

Ads never interrupt what you are doing. Rewarded ads are always your choice.

Privacy first

No account required. Your data stays on your device unless you choose cloud save. Each app has its own privacy policy.
