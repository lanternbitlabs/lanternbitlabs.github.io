Privacy Policy: Stepluma

Stepluma: Step Counter · Last updated: 8 November 2026 · Developer: Lanternbit Labs · Contact: lanternbit.labs@gmail.com

In short: Stepluma is a step counter with an optional "scroll time" feature that pauses the apps you choose until you walk. You don't need an account, and we have no servers. Your steps, weight, app usage and the apps you choose stay on your phone. Ads from Google AdMob only load if you choose to watch one.

  • None of this is sent to us. We have no servers and no way to see it.
  • Stepluma never reads what you see or type in other apps, and it does not use an accessibility service.
  • If you have turned on Android's device backup, Android may include your step history, goal and step length in your own Google backup, so they come back on a new phone. This is handled by Google under your Google account settings; we never receive it. Your weight and scroll time settings are deliberately left out.
  • Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

Permissions

  • Physical activity: to read your phone's step sensor.
  • Usage access (optional, scroll time only): to know which app is open and today's time in the apps you chose.
  • Display over other apps (optional, scroll time only): to show the pause screen.
  • Notifications (optional): to show that scroll time is on.

The app explains each one before Android asks, and the step counter works without the scroll time permissions.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Stepluma shows an ad only when you tap "Watch an ad" to get an extra rule change. When you do, Google AdMob may collect and use:

  • your device's advertising ID and app set ID
  • approximate location derived from your IP address
  • ad interaction data (for example, which ads were shown or tapped)
  • diagnostic and performance information

Google uses this to show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you are asked for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings → Ads and privacy → Privacy options. Stepluma never gives Google your steps, weight, chosen apps or app usage.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

More about how Google uses data: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Sharing achievements

When you tap Share on a medal, Stepluma makes a picture on your phone and hands it to Android's share sheet. You choose where it goes; Stepluma uploads nothing itself.

Children

Stepluma is not directed at children under 13.
